# About Filomena Propaganda

> Creative Communication, Digital Marketing, Branding, and Paid Media Agency based in São Carlos - SP, Brazil.

## Company Overview
Founded in 2013, Filomena Propaganda (Filomena Propaganda e Marketing Ltda) is a full-service creative and performance marketing agency. We specialize in helping established companies and ambitious startups build commanding brand presence, scale qualified lead generation, and drive revenue growth through data-driven campaigns.

## Core Capabilities
- **Branding & Visual Identity**: Market research, brand architecture, naming, logo design, complete design systems, and brandbooks.
- **Paid Traffic & Performance Marketing**: Full-funnel campaign management across Google Ads, Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook), and LinkedIn Ads focused on ROAS and low CAC.
- **Web Development & UI/UX**: Ultra-fast websites, institutional portals, and conversion-optimized landing pages built on modern static architectures.
- **Social Media & Content**: Editorial strategies, high-end motion graphics, short-form video production, and social copywriting.
- **Integrated Campaigns**: 360-degree brand launch and demand generation campaigns.
